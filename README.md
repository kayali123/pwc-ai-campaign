# Human judgment, on camera

A portfolio case study of Qiaochu Li's 2024 UCD summer practice proposal for PwC's EU AI advisory marketing.

## Project record

- **Context:** UCD MSc Digital Marketing team project, July–August 2024.
- **Role:** Five-person team lead and creative originator. Led the idea, channel plan, budget framework, measurement plan and final English presentation.
- **Actual outcome:** Highest mark in the programme; proposal presented to PwC's EU marketing team.
- **Status:** Academic proposal. PwC did not commission or run the campaign, and no commercial results are claimed.

## Case-study approach

The original proposal used a documentary format to make PwC's “human-led, tech-powered” positioning visible through an expert's handling of an AI problem. This portfolio version makes the buying question, illustrative scene and B2B journey easier to follow. It also separates the 2024 proposal from a 2026 reflection on how to test a live single-market pilot.

The €420k total was used in an earlier portfolio planning scenario; it is not claimed as PwC spend or as a verified figure from the 2024 academic presentation. The budget allocation on the page is an updated *planning* allocation, with an initial research and first-cut gate before the full series or heavier media spend. Its seven amounts sum to €420k and its displayed whole-number shares sum to 100%.

The four-frame storyboard is illustrative and was made for this case-study page. It depicts no real PwC employee, client work or produced campaign footage.
